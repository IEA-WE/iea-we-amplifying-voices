Amplifying Women's Voices

Making women's research heard.

Through the Amplifying Voices Project, IEA-WE works with women economists across Africa, Asia and Latin America to bring research into global policy conversations and expand their visibility and influence.

From research to global policy conversations.

The Amplifying Voices Project supports women economists in translating their academic research into accessible, policy-relevant writing and reaching audiences beyond academia.


IEA-WE has partnered with major platforms including Project Syndicate, VoxEU, VoxDev and IMF Finance & Development to help bring diverse voices and perspectives into economic debate.
