The initiative

Changing who gets heard.

The International Economic Association has launched a multi-year global initiative to strengthen the presence and influence of women in the economics profession.

Through research, collaboration, and advocacy, IEA-WE seeks to identify and remove barriers to women's participation — and to amplify the voices of women economists in shaping global economic discourse.

We believe that economics is stronger when more perspectives contribute to the questions the discipline asks, the evidence it produces, and the policies it informs.

Why this matters

Better economics needs more voices.

Economics shapes how we understand governments, markets, and public policy — yet women remain underrepresented, especially in leadership roles.

These disparities deepen when viewed through the lens of race, ethnicity, geography, caste and region. Women from developing countries can face a double disadvantage — by gender and geography.

IEA-WE recognises that progress requires both expanding the pipeline of qualified women economists and dismantling the structural barriers that hinder advancement.

Greater inclusion is not only a matter of fairness. Diverse perspectives lead to richer analyses, more innovative solutions, and ultimately better-functioning societies.
