01
Why this matters
→

A stronger economics profession starts with inclusion.

Economics shapes how we understand governments, markets, and public policy — yet women remain underrepresented, particularly in leadership roles. These disparities deepen across race, ethnicity, and geography, with women from developing countries often facing a double disadvantage.

IEA-WE takes an intersectional approach, examining how gender intersects with race, caste, and region to identify patterns of multiple disadvantage.

Diversity strengthens the discipline.

Greater inclusion isn't only a matter of fairness — diverse perspectives lead to richer analysis, more innovative solutions, and better-functioning societies.

A global community of women economists. Across Africa, Asia and Latin America, IEA-WE connects researchers, policymakers, and institutions working to strengthen women's participation and influence in economics.
02
Our work
→
01 — Quantitative Research

Building the evidence base.

Six evidence studies — across Argentina, Colombia, Ghana, India, Mexico and South Africa — have been completed and published, building a comprehensive foundation for understanding the structural barriers women economists face.

Alongside this, eleven intervention projects are underway testing practical strategies to close the gender gap and grow the number of women who choose economics as a field of study and career.

Explore the research →
02 — Qualitative Research

Understanding women's experiences.

In-depth interviews and focus groups tracking women economists' experience over time, in their own words. 220 in-depth interviews and 100+ focus group discussions completed so far, led by Monica Biradavolu of QualAnalytics.

03 — Amplifying Women's Voices

Making research heard.

Over 100 articles by 120+ women economists from 30 countries have reached a global audience of 18 million+, through partnerships with Project Syndicate, VoxEU, and VoxDev.

06
About IEA-WE
→

Advancing equity and excellence in economics.

The International Economic Association launched IEA-WE as a multi-year global initiative to strengthen the presence and influence of women in the economics profession.

Through research, collaboration and advocacy, the initiative works to expand the pipeline of women economists, remove structural barriers to their advancement, and amplify their voices in shaping global economic discourse.
