Why this matters

A stronger economics profession starts with inclusion.

Economics shapes how we understand governments, markets and public policy — yet women remain underrepresented, especially in leadership roles.

These disparities deepen when viewed through the lens of race, ethnicity and geography. Women from developing countries often face a double disadvantage — by gender and geography.

IEA-WE takes an intersectional approach, examining how gender intersects with race, caste and region to create patterns of multiple disadvantage.

Diversity strengthens the discipline.

Greater inclusion is not only a matter of fairness. Diverse perspectives lead to richer analyses, more innovative solutions and ultimately better-functioning societies.

A global community of women economists.

Across Africa, Asia and Latin America, IEA-WE connects researchers, policymakers and institutions working to strengthen women's participation and influence in economics.

About IEA-WE

Women shaping the future of economics.

The International Economic Association has launched a multi-year global initiative to strengthen the presence and influence of women in the economics profession.

Through research, collaboration and advocacy, the initiative seeks to identify and remove barriers to women's participation — and to amplify the voices of women economists in shaping global economic discourse.

The initiative brings together leading scholars committed to advancing equity and excellence in the field.
