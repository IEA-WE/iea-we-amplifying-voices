Research & Reports

Evidence for a more inclusive economics profession.

IEA-WE's research examines gender inequality in the economics profession, identifies barriers to women's participation and evaluates interventions that can help create lasting change.

Building the evidence base

Understanding the barriers — and what works.

IEA-WE's research spans Latin America, Africa and Asia and focuses on understanding gender gaps in the economics profession, documenting experiences and barriers, and identifying effective interventions.

The research programme brings together evidence from different countries and contexts to help strengthen the evidence base for greater gender equality in economics.
