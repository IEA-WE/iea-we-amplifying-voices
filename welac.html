Women in Economics in Latin America and the Caribbean

Building a stronger network of women economists across Latin America and the Caribbean.

WELAC advances gender equality in economics by building a strong network of women economists, supporting their professional development, and enhancing their visibility in academic and policy circles.

About WELAC

The WELAC Theory of Change aims to advance gender equality in economics by strengthening networks, creating opportunities for professional development, and increasing the visibility of women economists across academic and policy communities.

Co-Impact funding facilitated the hiring of Inés Berniell as WELAC Coordinator, working alongside WELAC Director Raquel Fernández, as well as the hiring of Milagros Onofri, who contributes to the management and coordination of WELAC activities.

Progress towards outcomes

2025 in focus

01

WELAC Survey

The second WELAC Survey gathered responses from 1,400 students across 96 universities in eight Latin American countries: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Peru and Uruguay. The survey explored future academic plans, job preferences, the influence of role models, and barriers to further study, including financial constraints, partner considerations and family obligations.

Preliminary results were presented by Raquel Fernández at the LACEA Annual Meeting in Recife in November 2025.

02

National Committees & WELAC Network

The WELAC network now includes six active national committees in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia and Uruguay. The committees conduct activities including workshops, mentoring sessions and advocacy events within their national economics associations.

03

WELAC-RIDGE Workshops

Two major workshops were held in 2025. The 5th RIDGE-WELAC Workshop on Gender and Household Economics took place in Lima, Peru, in May, receiving 93 submissions and featuring 14 selected papers.

The CAF-WELAC Workshop in Buenos Aires followed in December, receiving 180 submissions and featuring 16 accepted papers alongside distinguished invited speakers.

04

LACEA Special Session

A special WELAC session on Gender Economics: Policies and Cultural Norms was held at the LACEA Annual Meeting in Recife in November 2025. The session highlighted research on public policy, economic conditions, gender norms and behavioural outcomes.

05

Mentorship Programme

The annual WELAC mentorship session at the LACEA Annual Meeting brought together PhD students, junior faculty and senior economists to discuss the future direction of the profession, emerging research questions and changing priorities in economics.

The session drew 94 registered participants.

06

WELAC Directory

The Directory of Women Economists in Latin America and the Caribbean was successfully launched on the WELAC website. It now features more than 400 profiles of women economists across the region, supporting networking, visibility and participation in conferences, seminars and policy debates.
